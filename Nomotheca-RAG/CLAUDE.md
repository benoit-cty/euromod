# Nomotheca-RAG (legislation DB + ingest)

Loaded when working under `Nomotheca-RAG/`. These rules complete the repo-root [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), which keeps the cross-cutting constraints; paths in backticks are relative to the repo root.

- **Ingest: source quirks, reparse/rechunk, loader** (continues the root CLAUDE.md entry) Two source quirks are handled in the adapters: TAR never consolidates a never-amended act, so the LT adapter parses the Dokumentas row's own text as a `<id>/orig` version when the consolidation index is empty (the annual social-fund budget laws), and splits an act with no `straipsnis` — a Government resolution (nutarimas) or a ministerial order (įsakymas), where Lithuania sets the year's MMA, BSI, VRP, MVPD — into its numbered points, cited «2024 m. rugpjūčio 28 d. nutarimas Nr. 709 1 punktas» and keyed by `dokumento_id` (until 8 Nov 2026 they loaded as empty `istatymas` shells, and every MMA-based LT parameter was not_found with the resolution "ingested"); BOE's consolidated API has only the acts BOE consolidated — the yearly cotización orders (Orden ESS/55/2018, where the contribution bases and ceilings live) answer 404 there, so the ES adapter falls back to the daily-gazette XML (`/diario_boe/xml.php?id=`) and parses the act as published, one version per article from its `fecha_vigencia` (until 10 Nov 2026 every scout ingest of one failed), and the same API serves the autonomous communities' consolidated texts under their own gazette ids (`BOA-d-2005-90006`, Aragón's tax code, `BOCM-m-…`, `DOGV-r-…`, `BOCL-h-…`: the regional income-tax scales), which the adapter and the scout's id pattern accept since 18 Nov 2026 — only `BOE-A-…` passed before, so every regional scale the scout found was dropped; eISB serves Irish acts as enacted only, so the IE adapter also reads the Law Reform Commission's Revised Acts (source `IE-LRC`, `cli instrument ie 2005/act/26/revised`, `countries/ie/revised.py`): one HTML page per act, current state only, filed as its own instrument «Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005 (Revised)», each section starting at its latest commenced F-note date («Substituted (31.12.2025 to 7.01.2026)» → 7 January 2026, so the 2026 rate table is never offered as the 2025 one) — the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 is not published there, and a new revision needs a refetch; Irish statutory instruments are read as made from eISB's print page (`cli instrument ie 2007/si/142`, `countries/ie/si.py`, `instrument_type='statutory_instrument'`, since 19 Nov 2026): no XML and no structure markup there, so the text is cut after the enacting words on the regulations' own numbering 1., 2., 3. … and on a centred «SCHEDULE n», the explanatory note and signature left out — the amounts an Act leaves «as may be prescribed» (qualified-adult income limits, HAP rent limits) live there, and a later amendment or revocation is NOT marked on the older S.I. (`amendment_note.as_made`); BWB keeps the base figure an act was written with and prints the amount an indexation regeling put in force as an editors' note, which the NL parser keeps as `[Red: per 1 januari 2025: € 14,06]` (dropped until 24 Nov 2026: the minimum wage was proposed at its 2020 base), loads each `<bijlage>` as a unit («Besluit kinderopvangtoeslag, bijlage I», the toeslag table; annexes over 120 000 characters are registers and are skipped), and samples the toestanden of 1 January AND 1 July (`POLICY_DATES`), since WML, AKW, Participatiewet, AOW and Anw amounts change on both — EUROMOD NL holds the January amount, so those parameters are curated `start_of_year`; what a Minister only announces is not in BWB at all, so the NL adapter also reads the Staatscourant as published (source `NL-STCRT`, `cli instrument nl stcrt-2024-39288`, `countries/nl/staatscourant.py`, since 24 Nov 2026): the half-yearly «Mededeling … gewijzigde bedragen» is cut into its lettered sections («Stcrt. 2024, 39288, C Algemene Ouderdomswet»: the gross AOW pension, € 1.580,92), each repeating the notice's «met ingang van 1 januari 2025» sentence, a regulation into its articles and annexes, and the «Toelichting» is filed as a second instrument `<id>/toelichting` of class `guidance` (the maximum daily wage, «oftewel 290,67 euro», is stated only there) — only `<regeling>` and `<circulaire>` bodies are read, a later notice does NOT close an older one (`amendment_note.as_published`), and the scout accepts `stcrt-…` ids from officielebekendmakingen.nl; and BWB publishes some statutory formulas as PNG images (Participatiewet art. 22a), which the NL parser renders as `[formule als afbeelding: …]`, and withdraws toestanden by marking their item `_deleted="true"` in the manifest while serving the URL as a 301 to itself — `select_toestanden` skips them (until 23 Nov 2026 it picked the withdrawn `2025-01-01_0` over its live `_1` regeneration, and the scout's ingest of the Regeling zorgverzekering failed on TooManyRedirects). Every snapshot stores the `SourceRef` it was fetched for (`fetch_snapshots.metadata.source_ref`, since 16 Nov 2026), so `reparse` replays any country's archive; snapshots older than that are replayable for FR only (its URLs carry the id) — refetch the others. Archive-first pays off through `cli reparse <CC> --url-like …`: after a parser change it replays the stored snapshot bytes through parser and loader without refetching (the FR parser now keeps Legifrance's NOTA application note — «applicables à l'imposition des revenus de l'année 2025» — as a trailing `NOTA :` paragraph, which is where a consolidated article says which income year it applies to); changed texts leave stale vectors for `embeddings build` to refresh. `cli rechunk [CC]` re-chunks stored texts whose chunks stop short of their content, with no fetch and no parse. An empty text gets no chunk (a (0, 0) chunk broke `chunks_check` and failed the whole act: the scout's ingests of loi 84-53 and of several KALI conventions in the 5 Nov run), and an id the DILA mirror answers 404 fails as such, not as a JSONDecodeError. A closed version ends where an already-loaded later version of the unit begins (`loader._chain_versions`): a KALI 2019–2023 text over a 2022 amendment, or a LEGI version «jusqu'au 1er janvier 2027» over one in force since 31 Oct 2026, broke `legal_unit_versions_no_overlap` and failed the act. Until 3 Nov 2026 the loader's count-based cut dropped the last chunk of every re-loaded text over 6 000 chars, 814 texts in all; follow it with `embeddings build`.
- **Contributed documents: section parsing, editions, PDF fetching** (continues the root CLAUDE.md entry) In a `numbered` document, a heading must start with a letter after its number (a table row «19 212,03 € 1 601 €» is not section 19), and the numbered paragraph stays in its unit's content; a bare count with almost no body before a sibling heading is a table row, not a heading (`documents.drop_table_rows`: «1 enfant 0,0619% 0,0516%» … made ten 25-character sections of the Caf EAJE barème, cut off from the column headers), and bare numbers that start over are footnotes: only a first run reaching 2 numbers sections (Agirc-Arrco circular 2024-18 reads 1 | 1 2 3 | 1, and its 2025 rate table was a section opening on the footnote «1 Plafond de sécurité sociale.»; all 130 FR documents were reparsed and re-embedded on 7 Nov). A numbered section opens on the headings above it (`documents._section_units`): «I - …», «II - …» in sequence before any numbered section are parts, «2 - LE COMPLÉMENT FAMILIAL» is a heading, a heading with no text before its first sub-heading gets no unit text and is carried by its sub-sections, and a section under a part is cited «§II - 1.1 …» — instruction DSS/2B/2024/181 numbers Mayotte's sections from 1 again, and its ARS ceiling (35 004 €) was proposed as the national one (28 444 €) until 24 Nov 2026. Dated editions of one publication are chained (`contributed/series.py`, run after every contributed load and reparse): a BOFiP edition ends where the next edition held begins — BOI-RSA-BASE-30-30 was held as three open-ended documents (2012, 2016, 2017) that took 8 of 15 search results; the latest edition held stays open even when the law has moved on (that one still reads «5,1 points» of deductible CSG; the CGI says 6,8). After a parser change, `cli reparse-documents [-j CC] [--title-like …]` rebuilds contributed documents from their stored snapshot (units replaced, instrument and declaration kept; then `embeddings build`). Every unit's citation is unique: the loader matches units by citation, and before 6 Nov 2026 a column header repeated over several tables («0 à 3 ans 3 à 6 ans», now a table row, as is any section numbered 0) made the sections share one citation and keep only the first text — DSS/2B/2025/38 lost its ASF, complément familial and ARS amounts, 40 Cnav circulars and BOFiP barèmes lost text; all 119 FR documents were reparsed on 6 Nov. A contributed `.pdf` URL that answers httpx with an HTML page is retried once in headless Firefox (`contributed.fetch_in_browser`, optional `browser` extra; the worker image installs Firefox): sante.gouv.fr serves its Bulletin officiel — instructions DSS/2B/2025/38 (BMAF) and DSS/2B/2024/181 (family-benefit ceilings) — to a browser and a «Vérification de sécurité» page to a script; a page that wants a human is left to the human, and when no browser gets the PDF the ingest is refused, never fed the HTML page (the AGS 2025 rate PDF answered with the press-release listing, ingested as the release). A scanned PDF has no text layer and there is no OCR: contribute a Markdown transcription instead (the ANI APEC du 12 juillet 2011, art. 14: 0,036 / 0,024 pour 100).
- **Embeddings on the target workstation (Intel Ultra 7 265H) prefer OpenVINO over ONNX** on the local CPU path. On the NVIDIA box (GTX 1080 Ti) OpenVINO is not an option at all — its GPU plugin is Intel-only — so that machine runs `--backend torch` on CUDA from a separate environment (`UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT=$PWD/.venv-cuda uv sync --package nomotheca-legislation-ingest --extra embeddings-cuda` from `Nomotheca-RAG/ingest` — an absolute path, since in the workspace a relative one resolves from the repo root; cu126 wheels because Pascal is sm_61, fp32 because Pascal fp16 is 1/64 rate). The workspace `.venv` stays CPU+OpenVINO so the UI and `scout.py` keep working. BGE-M3 with the `fix_mistral_regex` flag is a known breakage — leave it off (details in [Nomotheca-RAG/ingest/README.md](../Nomotheca-RAG/ingest/README.md)).
- **The embedding model is a registry id, and a query must be encoded by the model that embedded the corpus.** `nomotheca_ingest/core/embedding_models.py` is the one list (1 = BGE-M3, the default; 2 = Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, 5 = Arctic-Embed-L v2, …; 99 stays the in-SQL placeholder): each model's vectors live under its own `embeddings.model_id` in the one `halfvec(1024)` column (Matryoshka truncation above 1024, zero-padding below), and its query prompt lives there too — `SentenceTransformerBackend.encode_queries` applies it, `query_encoder.encode(query, url, model_id)` and the worker's encode lane (`model_id` in the payload) route to it. Compare models with `embeddings build --model <key> --authentic-only --in-force-on …` then `nomokrisis-eval build-embedding-cases` (golden cases → retrieval cases with the `frame` step's own query, `pipeline.frame_query`), `run-embeddings --embedding-model-id N`, `compare-embeddings`. A vector query that orders by `embedding <=> q` next to filters walks the HNSW index and filters the 40 global nearest afterwards (2 rows out of 15 for LT): rank inside a window function or by a computed score. Hybrid search is one SQL function, `search_chunks()` (`Nomotheca-RAG/db/schema.sql`, migration 0004): the workflow's `retrieval.hybrid_search`, the eval's `vector_search` (FTS leg off), the review UI's and RAG chat's `db::search::search` and the MCP server all call it — live version, trust class, OR-of-terms FTS, RRF plus the vector top-3 slot in one place.
