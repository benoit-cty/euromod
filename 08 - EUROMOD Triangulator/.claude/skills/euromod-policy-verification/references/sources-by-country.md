# Source Patterns by Country

## Slovenia

- Legislation: https://pisrs.si (requires JS — often inaccessible; try direct PDF links from search)
- Health insurance homepage: https://www.zzzs.si (links to subportals — do not fetch subportals directly, they are JS-rendered)
- Social benefits (income support): https://www.gov.si/teme/varstveni-dodatek/
- Social benefits (birth grant): https://www.gov.si/teme/pomoc-ob-rojstvu-otroka/ *(check URL — may change)*
- Social benefits (other): https://www.gov.si/teme/ + [specific slug] (always use the topic-specific page, not the homepage)
- Tax authority (FURS — PIT): https://www.furs.gov.si/dohodnina ⚠️ NOT `fu.gov.si` — that domain fails
- Ministry of Education (childcare fees): https://www.mizs.gov.si (often inaccessible — try specific subpages)
- Country Report: `Y16_CR_SI.pdf` or `Y16_CR_SI.docx` (EUROMOD version J2.0) — both formats may be present; use whichever is available
