Social Security Contributions –
Working in the EU

The EU social security coordination rules determine applicable legislation for cross-border situations. Employers and self-employed individuals can use the Self-Assessment Tool to assess their social security obligations in Malta when working across EU countries, receiving personalized guidance based on their specific circumstances.
