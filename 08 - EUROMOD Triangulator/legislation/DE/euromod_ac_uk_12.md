Research

Cross-cutting research on distributional issues, from tax & benefit systems to family & gender, health & wellbeing, population change & labour market dynamics

See our research »

EUROMOD

EUROMOD enables researchers and analysts to calculate, in a comparable manner across countries, the effects of taxes and benefits on household incomes and work incentives

UKMOD

UKMOD permits analysis of the effects of taxes and social benefits on household incomes and work incentives for the population of each nation in the UK

JAS-mine

JAS-mine is a Java platform that aims at providing a unique simulation tool for discrete-event simulations, including agent-based and dynamic microsimulation models
