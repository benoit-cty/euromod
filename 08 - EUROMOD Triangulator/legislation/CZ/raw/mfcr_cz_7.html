Budgetary Frameworks

Fiscal data and other information required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility (resp. Directive 2011/85/EU on requirements for Budgetary Frameworks of the Member States).

  1. Home
  2. Fiscal policy
  3. Budgetary Frameworks

Budgetary Frameworks

Release calendar

Publication dates of statistical information required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll. on the website of the Ministry of Finance.

Statistical Information

Fiscal data and other information required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility.

Public sector

List of institutional units classified according to the REGULATION (EU) No 549/2013 of the EP and of the Council on the European system of national and regional accounts in the EU (ESA) in the general government, and the units controlled by the general government (public corporations) - see the register administered by the CZSO pursuant to Act No 89/1995 Coll., the State Statistical Service.

Fiscal data

Cash-based fiscal data (or the equivalent figure from public accounting if cash-based data are not available) as required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility.

Tax expenditures

Detailed information on the impact of tax expenditures on revenues of general government – as required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility.

Shares and other equities held by government

Information on participation of general government in the capital of private and public corporations in respect of economically significant amounts – as required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility.

Contingent liabilities and other information

Information for all subsectors of general government on contingent liabilities with potentially large impact on public budgets, including government guarantees, non-performing loans and liabilities stemming from the operation of public corporations – as required by the Act No 23/2017 Coll., on the rules of budgetary responsibility.
