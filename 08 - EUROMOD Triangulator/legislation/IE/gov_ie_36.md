M4-M1 Commuter Belt Area

These limits apply as follows, with all other areas subject to the relevant county limits:

For

Meath

, the area associated with the towns of Ashbourne, Dunboyne, Dunshaughlin and Ratoath along with the area south of Drogheda and north of Fingal ranging from Stamullen, Julianstown and Laytown.

For

Louth

, the area associated with Drogheda Town and its environs.

For

Kildare

, the towns previously incorporated into the original North Kildare limits (Kilcock; Maynooth; Leixlip; and Celbridge)

Wicklow M11 Commuter Towns

These limits apply to the towns of Bray, Delgany, Greystones and Wicklow only.  All other areas in Wicklow are subject to the Wicklow limits.​​​​​​​​
