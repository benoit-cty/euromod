Applying for insurance

You can apply for insurance once you have received an affirmative grant or scholarship decision.

Foreign recipients of Finnish grants and scholarships

If you are coming to Finland to work on a grant or scholarship, you must check whether you have an obligation to insure your grant or scholarship work.

Mela sickness allowance for recipients of grants

Has your grant work been affected by illness? Mela sickness allowance is paid as compensation for loss of income due to work incapacity caused by an illness.
