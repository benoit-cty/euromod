National accounts by institutional sectors 2016–2019

A A A
Date of publication: 30.07.2021
Frequency of publication: annual edition

The publication is the elaboration of Polish national accounts by institutional sectors. The publication includes detailed national accounts by institutional sectors for the years 2016-2019.

Data has been compiled according to European System of National and Regional Accounts in the European Union (ESA 2010) introduced by Regulation No 549/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council (EU) of 21 May 2013.

The publication presents tables with nonfinancial accounts by the institutional sectors and by NACE rev.2. Tables contain data such as: gross domestic product (GDP), gross national income (GNI), households final consumption expenditure, net lending or net borrowing in general government sector. Subjects of hidden economy and illegal activity in national accounts and gross domestic product at purchasing power parity are also presented in this publication.

The publication contains the methodological solutions, definitions and terms used in national accounts, moreover graphs and the economic interpretation of the national economy. Tabular data sweet is included In a separate Excel file.
By: institutional sectors, institutional sub-sectors, types of activity
