DESCRIPTION

I-CUE feasibility studies explore the feasibility of introducing EUROMOD in the New Member States, and thus provide the basis for enlarging the current EUROMOD which covers the 15 member states pre May 2004. Queries should be directed to the authors of the report. Most Country Reports contain sections on

key features of national tax-benefit systems;

identification of appropriate data sources which meet the data requirements for tax/benefit-microsimulation

considerations of specific modelling issues (e.g. tax evasion, non take-up of benefits, etc.).
